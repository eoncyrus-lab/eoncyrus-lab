## Investment research, supported by software

I am a long-horizon investor. I read primary sources, compare competing arguments, and build small systems to collect, structure, and revisit evidence. Code, agents, and automation are infrastructure for judgment—not the point by themselves.

<strong>Currently building:</strong> a personal research stack for source curation, market intelligence, and repeatable investment decisions.

## Research stack

| Layer | Project | Role in the research process |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary sources | [Financial Freedom](https://github.com/eoncyrus-lab/Financial_freedom) | Curated value-investing, Bitcoin, and monetary research with competing views preserved. |
| Signal intake | [Nimbus News](https://github.com/eoncyrus-lab/nimbus-news) | Collects market news, reports, prices, and LLM-assisted briefings. |
| Analysis tools | [iWenCai SkillHub Tools](https://github.com/eoncyrus-lab/iwencai-skillhub-tools) | Turns finance research capabilities into reusable AI-assisted workflows. |
| Publishing | [WeChat MD Vintage](https://github.com/eoncyrus-lab/wechat-md-vintage) | Converts research notes into restrained, readable long-form publishing. |
| Infrastructure | [singbox-deploy](https://github.com/eoncyrus-lab/singbox-deploy) | Keeps the personal research environment reproducible and dependable. |

## Investment principles

- **Primary sources first** — prefer filings, letters, transcripts, and original research over summaries.
- **Preserve opposing views** — a useful research system should make disconfirming evidence easy to find.
- **Tools serve judgment** — automation should improve attention and traceability, not replace thinking.
- **Long-term compounding** — capital, knowledge, and decision quality improve through repeated good process.
